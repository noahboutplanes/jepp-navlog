Fill in checkpoints, altitude, winds aloft (true), temp, TC, variation (e.g. 7W or +7) and leg distances. Altitude, winds, temp, variation and deviation carry down to the next leg until you change them. TAS, GPH and % power come from the selected model's POH cruise table at your cruise RPM, pressure altitude (departure altimeter) and temperature. KOSU frequencies and field elevation fill in automatically when KOSU is the departure or destination (FAA data eff. Feb 2026 — verify against the current Chart Supplement). A leg that climbs above the previous altitude uses the POH time/fuel/distance-to-climb table from the departure Field Elev. Start/taxi fuel per POH is taken before leg 1. Enter ATAs as you fly and the ETAs, ATEs and actual ground speeds update. Everything is calculated on this page; nothing is fetched.
