# Jepp VFR Nav Log — OSU C172 fleet

A fillable Jeppesen VFR navigation log that calculates as you type. Pick an OSU tail (172S/R/P), enter checkpoints, altitude, winds aloft, temperature, TC, variation and leg distances, and it fills in CAS/TAS, WCA, TH, MH, CH, GS, ETE, ETA, fuel burn and remaining, plus POH climb, cruise RPM performance, weight & balance and takeoff/landing distances.

Everything runs in the browser from built-in POH tables; nothing is fetched. KOSU frequencies auto-fill when KOSU is the departure or destination.

**Planning aid only.** Not a substitute for the aircraft POH/AFM, official weather and NOTAM briefings, or your instructor. Verify every value before flight.
